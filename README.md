# Sidney Scott

**Builder · Investor · AI Researcher**

I build and measure systems at the boundary between digital intelligence and the physical world.

My work connects **AI economics, inference infrastructure, and Cognitive & World Models**. I study how physical constraints shape the cost and deployment of intelligence, and how machines learn to represent and act on the world. The public work here connects research questions to reproducible measurements and working infrastructure.

## Open work

### [Reckon](https://github.com/reckonresearch/reckon)

Adaptive placement infrastructure for AI inference. **berth** predicts workload cost and latency; **sounding** independently measures the real system; **pilot** adapts placement as conditions change, within a declared autonomy policy.

The goal is to make inference decisions in terms of useful work delivered under latency and quality constraints, not simply GPU-hours or benchmark throughput.

[Code](https://github.com/reckonresearch/reckon) · [Documentation](https://docs.reckonresearch.com/) · [Defect register](https://github.com/reckonresearch/reckon/blob/main/DEFECTS.md)

### [The Mismatch Tax](https://github.com/reckonresearch/mismatch-tax)

The pre-registered empirical companion to *The Mismatch Tax*: comparing delivered decode throughput with a declared ceiling on the Reckon measured corpus. Hypotheses, measurement provenance, analysis code, and results are public so the claims can be examined and reproduced.

[Code](https://github.com/reckonresearch/mismatch-tax) · [Results](https://github.com/reckonresearch/mismatch-tax/blob/main/RESULTS.md) · [Paper](https://www.sidney.fyi/papers/the-mismatch-tax.pdf)
